omega-zero UI

The kit omega-zero's menus are drawn from: pixel-art components rebuilt in HTML as the reference for each screen, before and while it is drawn on the device. Every shape is a sprite written pixel by pixel, every word lands on whole pixels, and every colour follows the backdrop colour the player picks in Settings › Theme › Colour, over the pattern picked in Style. The rules for adding to the kit are in assets/ui/README.md.

Foundation

The art pixel

foundation/tokens.css

One art pixel is --u, 2px: omega-zero draws its pixel art 2×2, so a 640×480 panel is 320×240 art pixels. Every size in every component is a whole number of them, and nothing is ever smoothed: sprites are shown with image-rendering: pixelated.

Sprites

foundation/pixel.js

A shape is written in its component as rows of colour keys, one character to an art pixel, and OmegaPixel.sprite() paints it. A shape that must fit any size is a 9-slice: only its top-left quarter is drawn, mirror() makes the rest, and its corners stay as drawn while its edges stretch.

const CORNER = [       // omega-modal's frame
  '...kk',             // k dark, H bright, s stripe,
  '.kkHH',             // A frame colour, . nothing
  '.kHss',
  'kHsAA',
  'kHsAA',
];

Type

foundation/fonts.css

Pixel Code Regular at 18px, one face pixel to an art pixel, on a 20px line so its capitals sit on whole pixels. Pixel Code Bold is fattened off the grid, so it is not used: bold is drawn by setting the word again one pixel to the right. Bigger words (an action's label) are drawn into a sprite with OmegaPixel.letters() and stretched there, never with a CSS transform. Components draw it with text-rendering: geometricPrecision: the browser's default hinting smears its edges by part of a pixel.

Backdrop

foundation/backdrop.js

The shelf's backdrop: the backdrop colour under one of the patterns in Settings › Theme › Background, each a tile drawn in whole art pixels and drifting left with data-moving (16px a second, Bits 10), as crates/omega-ui/src/backdrop.rs draws them. The Style picked above puts the same pattern behind every example on this page.

APIWhat it does
.omega-backdropDraws the backdrop over the element, in the colour palette.js set.
data-stylegrid (without one), plates, dotted, dither, rivets, bits or solid.
data-movingDrifts the pattern left, as the device does with Motion on Moving.
<div class="omega-backdrop" data-style="rivets" data-moving></div>

Colour

foundation/palette.js

The palette is made from the backdrop colour. Its ramp is Mega Man Zero's own menu colours, which is what Amber gives; any other colour keeps how light each step is and turns the ramp to its own hue, taking as much colour as it has itself. Night, the default, gives plain greys. OmegaPalette.apply(name) sets these properties and every component draws itself again. The swatches follow the colour picked above.

Components

<omega-modal>

components/omega-modal.js

The menu window and nothing else: a double-lined frame that stacks what it is given top to bottom. It knows nothing of what it holds; usually that is <omega-panel>s, which grow to share its height and share one stripe where they meet. It fills the box the page gives it.

APIWhat it does
slotAnything, stacked top to bottom. Give a child a bigger flex-grow (or a flex-basis in whole art pixels) to make it taller.
::partframe, content
<omega-modal>
  <omega-panel label="BUTTON SETTING" active></omega-panel>
  <omega-panel label="ATTACK MODE"></omega-panel>
</omega-modal>

<action-left>

components/action-left.js

The bottom bar's left action: a return arrow in a notch at the screen's edge, a big label, and a button pill with a hint. 15 art pixels tall; the notch rises 4 above it. Without attributes it is empty, with room kept for its label.

empty with text given room, as wide as CONFIRM A beside it
APIWhat it does
labelThe big word: a sprite, bold, its capitals 10 pixels tall, with a shadow.
fontbold (or none) or thin: the label drawn again a pixel to the right, or in Pixel Code's own strokes. See The bar's font.
hintThe smaller word in the pill.
roomHow wide the label's box and the hint's box are at least, in art pixels: 54 30. Each word stays in the middle of its box, rounded down to a whole pixel. <omega-bar> sets it on both its actions.
::partaction, icon, label, word, button, hint

<omega-row>

components/omega-row.js

One setting in a panel's well: its name on the left, its value on the right, 13 art pixels tall, as tall as a panel's header. The row under the cursor is lit the way a title tab is drawn, a band in the tab colour inside a frame line with its corner pixels left out, its name cream and its value bright. A value Left and Right change gets a ◀ and a ▶ while lit, a row that opens another screen a › at its end. Without any of that it is a plain name and value line. Five rows and four fit, with their two headers, in the modal of a 640×480 screen.

in a modal as big as a 640×480 screen's
APIWhat it does
labelThe setting's name, from the left, in capitals whatever case it is given in; cut off when it runs into the value.
valueWhat it is set to, from the right, as given. Optional.
litThe row under the cursor: the band, the name in --oz-cream, the value and arrows in --oz-frame-hi. Unlit, the name is --oz-label and the value --oz-dim.
caretsA ◀ and ▶ either side of the value while lit, for a value Left and Right change. Their room is kept unlit too, so the value never moves.
opensA › at the end, lit or not, for a row that opens another screen.
dimNot available: everything drawn in --oz-band, --oz-dim when lit, and no carets.
::partrow, label, caret-left, value, caret-right, opens
<omega-panel label="CONFIGS" active>
  <omega-row label="FAST FORWARD" value="6X" lit carets></omega-row>
  <omega-row label="CONTROLS" opens></omega-row>
  <omega-row label="SAVE SLOT" value="AUTO" carets dim></omega-row>
</omega-panel>

<omega-row label="IP" value="192.168.1.20"></omega-row>   <!-- a plain line -->

<omega-list-row>

components/omega-list-row.js

A Wi-Fi network in a list: an <omega-row> whose name keeps its own case, with what the network is to the handheld on the right, then a padlock if it needs a password and the top bar's Wi-Fi icon, as many of its three parts lit as the signal is strong. Room is kept for the padlock and the bars on every row, so they line up down the list. Lit and dim as <omega-row>.

APIWhat it does
labelThe network's name, from the left, in its own case.
valueWhat it is to the handheld, right-aligned before the icons: CONNECTED, JOINING, SAVED. Optional.
signal0 to 3: the Wi-Fi bars, that many of their three parts lit (dot, small arc, big arc), the rest faint. No bars without it.
lockA padlock beside the bars, for a network with a password.
lit, dimAs <omega-row>'s. The icons are drawn in the name's colour.
::partrow, label, value, lock, signal
<omega-list-row label="Home" value="CONNECTED" signal="3" lock lit></omega-list-row>
<omega-list-row label="Café Guest" signal="2"></omega-list-row>

<omega-bar>

components/omega-bar.js

The bar along the bottom of a screen, as wide as what holds it and 15 art pixels tall: an <action-left> and an <action-right> at its ends and, between them, nothing but the keys a screen has beyond the two actions, so whatever is behind the bar shows through. The two actions come out one width: the bar gives both the room of the wider label and the wider hint, and an empty one takes the other's. The keys go between them as hints, each its key in a small dark cap and then the word, centred between the actions and on the same rows as the words in the buttons.

empty one action, the empty one as wide with a hint with two
APIWhat it does
hintA key and what it does, the first word the key: Y FORGET, START FETCH. Two with a | between: Y FORGET | X EDIT.
fontbold (or none) or thin, given to both actions: see The bar's font.
slotleft for the <action-left>, right for the <action-right>.
::partbar, middle (the room between the actions), hints, hint, key, word
<omega-bar hint="Y FORGET | X EDIT">
  <action-left slot="left" label="BACK" hint="B"></action-left>
  <action-right slot="right" label="JOIN" hint="A"></action-right>
</omega-bar>

The bar's font

omega-bar font="thin"

Settings › Theme › Font picks how the actions' labels are drawn. Bold, the default, draws each letter again a pixel to its right; thin draws Pixel Code's own one-pixel strokes, a pixel a letter narrower, over the same shadow and with the same 10 rows of capitals. font on the bar gives it to both actions, which still come out one width: the shelf's two 107 art pixels wide in thin and 115 in bold, a menu's 90 and 97.

bold thin 
<omega-bar font="thin">
  <action-left slot="left" label="RETURN" hint="B"></action-left>
  <action-right slot="right" label="CONFIRM" hint="A"></action-right>
</omega-bar>
